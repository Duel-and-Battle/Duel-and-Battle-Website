Who We Are

About Duel and Battle

Founded in May 2024, Duel and Battle is an independent trading card business focused on delivering authentic, accurately graded singles for Yu‑Gi‑Oh!, Pokémon, and Magic: The Gathering players.

We manage every part of the process in‑house—sourcing, grading, listing, packaging, and customer support—so nothing is left to chance. This control allows us to maintain strict quality standards while ensuring that what you see is exactly what you receive.

Our approach is simple: prioritize accuracy, protect authenticity, and deliver a smooth buying experience every time. Combined with TCGPlayer’s Buyer Safeguard program, customers can shop with confidence knowing their order is handled with care, precision, and accountability from start to finish.

  • Focused inventory across Yu‑Gi‑Oh!, Pokémon, and Magic: The Gathering singles.
  • What you see is what you receive—no inflated condition claims.
  • Competitive pricing without compromising accuracy or quality.
  • Reliable fulfillment built for collectors and competitive players alike.
Core Values — Authenticity · Accuracy · Satisfaction
